# ZombieDefend

Phaser 3 horizontal tower-defense demo.

## Play

- **Demo (GitHub Pages):** https://peterlei853.github.io/ZombieDefend/
- Local: `npm start` then open http://localhost:5173
- **Sandbox weapons (v0.36.15):** `/sandbox/weapons.html?weapon=water` (also `sling`, `bubble`, `lightning`, `frost`, `flame`) floats that gun in front of the girl at its 56×56 catalog size and fires its projectile into a living target. Keys 1–6 switch guns. Water keeps `fx-water-proj`. It aims ahead of a marching body and keeps guiding onto a living body still ahead, so the opaque cores meet. A shot whose guide is gone keeps that heading until it hits or leaves the camera — it is not dropped on the stage spawn line while it is still on screen. On contact it plays `fx-lightning-impact` at the point and does not play splash, wet-ground, or other water hit sheets, and it does not chain. The page does not draw a tower or fence cradle. Stage mounts, gold, saves, barricade HP, and the five-stage roster stay as they are.

## Controls (demo slice)

- Boot → pick **Stage 1–5** (or Start). Each stage spawns one monster: office worker, delivery, chef, nurse, then brute.
- Survive 90s waves; barricade HP → 0 is Game Over
- Clear the field after 90s → shopping list (buy a gun for 40 gold, or upgrade an owned gun for 25) → Next Stage, through stage 5. After stage 5 the button returns to the title. Stage 1 still grants all six guns, so that list is mostly upgrades. A later stage opened from the title with no owned guns yet fields those same six. Gold on the list is the gold earned in the stage.
- **Move:** W / S or up / down arrows. A / D and left / right only change walk facing; the defender stays on the barricade lane.
- **Weapon:** The girl stays unarmed. Owned guns float in a front arc and fire only after she stops (180ms, then `player-girl-deploy`). Shots travel west along her forward ray. Stage 1 grants all six: water, sling, bubble, lightning, frost, flame. SPACE still cycles the highlighted gun. - **Pets:** four 64×32 bars sit top-right (phoenix fire, panda ice, lion electric, dragon poison), each with three skill orbs. Tap a bar to summon that baby behind you; a GO! float sits beside them. Tap again to recall — Back! shows the same way, and the pet walks to an empty sleep spot behind the barricade, plays sleep-start once, then loops sleep. Tap again to wake them and walk back out (no teleport). There is no deploy cooldown and no on-field time limit. While out, the pet walks off the hero's shot line (about 15% of the lane) unless several zombies are piled on the wall, in which case it taunts the ones nearest the fence. Its unlocked orb fills, flashes, then auto-casts. Recalled pets regen HP. At 0 HP the bar greys for a revive cooldown.
- **Phone:** joystick bottom-left (same axes), **SWAP** bottom-right (same as SPACE). Desktop keyboard still works.

### Try it on a phone

1. Open the demo URL, or run `npm start` and visit `http://<this-machine>:5173` from the phone (same network).
2. Use landscape. Start Stage 1.
3. Drag the bottom-left stick up and down to change lanes. Tap **SWAP** to switch weapons. Keyboard on a desktop should still move and switch weapons.
