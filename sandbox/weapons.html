Charge sheets: blue fx-water-charge-aura, yellow fx-water-charge-aura-l2 at 1.8s, red fx-water-charge-aura-l3 at 4s. L2 flies at 1.5× L1 speed. The 512×256 sheet, 8 frames @ 10, is drawn at 1.0× depth scale and the hit box matches. Direction stays the spawn vector. Overlapping an enemy slows that L3 shot to 0.4× its spawn speed until the shot is clear. Each tick retreats 5px toward spawn. At rest she holds the last frame of player-girl-deploy. The white X stays the 32×32 sheet drawn at 2×. This page does not publish GitHub Pages.
